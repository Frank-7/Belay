01 THE MOMENT THAT MATTERS

The request went out.
The answer didn’t
make it back.

Did the action fail?
Or did just the reply fail?

For an agent moving money or changing a system, that distinction matters. A retry can create a second action. A changed decision can lose the original intent.

Belay explores a different discipline: give the action a durable identity before the decision, then recover against that same identity.

Read the execution contract ↗
⌁
BEFORE THE ACTION

Anchor the intent.

Give an effect its identity before the agent decides. A restart shouldn’t invent a new action.

⌕
AFTER AN INTERRUPTION

Follow the evidence.

Look for what happened to the original operation. A model’s conclusion still needs verification.

⏸
AT THE NEXT STEP

Know when to stop.

Recheck permission before a new effect. If the outcome cannot be established, keep it unknown.

A RESULT WORTH SAYING OUT LOUD

The model didn’t change its decision.

Five separately measured 50-response samples found no within-sample decision disagreement. We kept the null result. Measuring how often decisions vary is a different question from recovering an uncertain action.
